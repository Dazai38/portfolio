What do you offer? +
Long-form YouTube editing, short-form content (Reels, Shorts, TikToks), and YouTube strategy — hooks, structure and packaging. Whether it's a full video or a batch of shorts, I bring your story to life with precision and creativity.
How do we get started? +
Book a quick call or DM me on X or Instagram. We'll talk about your channel, your goals and your style — then I'll take a first project so you can see how I work before committing to anything bigger.
What if the edit isn't what I imagined? +
We iterate. I'm always open to feedback and revisions are part of the process — the video isn't done until you're happy to hit publish.
How fast is the turnaround? +
It depends on the length and complexity of the project, but I'll give you a clear delivery date up front — and I keep it.
